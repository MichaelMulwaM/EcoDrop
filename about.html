About EcoDrop

EcoDrop is a community-driven neighborhood recycling and e-waste exchange directory built to keep reusable materials out of landfills.


We connect local residents who have surplus items—such as old electronics, cardboard boxes, furniture, and scrap metal—with neighbors who can put them to good use.


How it works: Browse listings, check pickup details, and reach out directly to arrange a safe, sustainable handover in your local area.
